7. FAQ

Section Title:
Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Do you only serve clients in Karnataka?
A: While most consultations are in Kannada and focused on Karnataka, online Vastu consultations are available for clients anywhere in India and abroad.

Q2: What do I need to prepare for an online consultation?
A: A clear floor plan with directions marked (North, East, South, West), photos of key areas (entrance, kitchen, bedrooms), and a list of your main concerns.

Q3: Are your remedies very expensive or structural?
A: Most suggestions are practical and low-cost: changes in placement, colors, small adjustments, and habits. Major structural changes are recommended only when truly needed.

Q4: Is this based on faith or science?
A: Vastu Shastra is a traditional system based on faith and ancient principles. The suggestions are meant to support your belief system and create a more harmonious living environment.

Q5: In which languages are consultations available?
A: Primarily in Kannada. Depending on availability, limited support in English/Hindi may be possible—please inquire while booking.
